# 005 – Spread view and page operations

Status: todo · Depends on: 004

## What

Show the Album as a strip of Spreads and let the user manage Pages. The selected Page opens in the editor canvas.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Spread strip pairs Pages according to the Album's starting side, with Page 1 alone on the chosen side.
- [ ] Pages can be added, deleted, duplicated and drag-reordered, and the Spreads re-pair correctly.
- [ ] The editor canvas renders the selected Page at the Page Format's aspect ratio, with guides for the trim edge, the Bleed and the Safe Zone.
- [ ] Page operations are domain-core functions with unit tests.
